# Five interview packets

These are five **preparation sheets**, not interview records. No interview findings are recorded
here. Use the common questions in [`interview-guide.md`](interview-guide.md) with every participant
so that responses remain comparable. Select one follow-up track only after learning about the
participant's actual experience; do not assign a role based on their name or make the focus prompt
sound like an assumed problem.

Use anonymous codes `I-01` through `I-05` in the public repository. Keep the name-to-code mapping
outside the repository. Four names were supplied for the five planned interviews; `I-05` remains
unassigned until a fifth participant is identified. The participant tracker remains “not
scheduled” until each person confirms a time.

## I-01 — Recent circle workflow

**Use if:** the participant has recently organized or joined an informal rotating savings group.

**Follow-up prompts:**

- Walk me through the last circle from the first invitation to the last payout.
- Who maintained the member list and payout order? How did the rest of the group check it?
- What did you do when someone was unavailable or the schedule changed?

**Capture:** actual recent steps, tools used, handoffs, and any concrete coordination incident.

## I-02 — Contribution visibility

**Use if:** the participant has personally checked or recorded who contributed.

**Follow-up prompts:**

- Tell me about the last time you needed to know whether a contribution had arrived.
- Where did you look, and how did you decide the record was reliable?
- What did you do when two people had different records?

**Capture:** the source of truth, checking effort, correction process, and the participant's own
confidence in the record.

## I-03 — Delays and exceptions

**Use if:** the participant has experienced a late contribution, a member leaving, or a delayed
payout.

**Follow-up prompts:**

- What happened most recently when a contribution was late or a member could not participate?
- Who decided what to do, and how was that decision communicated?
- What happened to the payout order and the group's trust afterward?

**Capture:** the event and resolution, without collecting amounts or personal financial details.

## I-04 — Trust and responsibility

**Use if:** the participant has firsthand experience with how a group's money is held or moved.

**Follow-up prompts:**

- How was responsibility for the pool decided in the most recent group?
- What made that arrangement feel acceptable or uncomfortable?
- What information would you need before trusting a different arrangement?

**Capture:** the participant's own trust criteria and examples. Do not suggest that blockchain or
non-custody necessarily improves trust.

## I-05 — Wallet and transaction comprehension

**Use if:** the participant has used a digital wallet or has tried a blockchain transaction;
otherwise ask about their first expectations without assuming experience.

**Follow-up prompts:**

- Tell me about the last time you connected a wallet or approved a digital transaction.
- What did you check before approving it? Was anything unclear?
- What would help you understand why a contribution, payout, or reclaim is currently unavailable?

**Capture:** observed past behavior separately from expectations about the Halka prototype.
